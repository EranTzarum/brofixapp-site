How Brofix works for a pro

No chasing stale leads and no back-and-forth phone calls before a customer picks your quote.

  1. Sign up and pick your trades and cities

    Create your profile, select your service categories and cities, and toggle your availability whenever you are ready to take jobs. For licensed trades (electrical, gas and pest control), upload your license for review before receiving requests.

  2. Get matching requests with photos and area

    When a customer in your service cities posts a request in your trade, you see the problem description, up to 3 photos, the city and approximate area (about 1 km), and an automatically calculated travel time.

  3. Send your price and departure time

    Want the job? Send a price quote in ILS, pick when you can leave (now, in 30 minutes, in an hour, or another time), and add an optional short note. The system calculates the estimated arrival time for the customer.

  4. If chosen: exact address, chat and live arrival

    Only after the customer selects your quote do they share their exact address and phone number, and a text chat opens to coordinate. Tap "Heading out" when leaving, open Waze or Google Maps to navigate, and tap "Arrived" at the door.

  5. Get paid directly and rate each other

    When the work is finished, tap "Done" and the customer confirms. If they don't respond, the job is confirmed automatically after 24 hours, and then both sides rate each other. The customer pays you directly (cash, Bit, or as agreed) outside the app, and either side can log the payment method and amount in the app as a record. If the job on site turns out larger or needs a return visit, send a change request for the customer to approve in the app.

Electrical, gas and pest control: license required

These trades need a valid license or certificate. You upload it in the app, and the Brofix team checks it. Until your license is checked and approved, you get no requests in that trade and cannot quote in it.

Some other trades, such as renovation contractor, roof waterproofing and tiling, are approved manually by the team before you get requests.

Why join Brofix

Built for independent tradespeople who want straightforward jobs and full control over their schedule and pricing.

You set the price and departure time

There is no fixed price list forced on you. You review each request, quote your own price, and choose when you can head out. Busy on another job? Flip your availability toggle off or skip requests that do not fit.

Two-sided ratings and a "verified license" tag

Both sides rate each other after every completed job, and you see the customer's rating before you quote. When the Brofix team checks and approves your license document for a trade, your quotes in that trade get a "verified license" tag. The tag means the Brofix team checked the document at the time of the check.

Privacy for professionals

Live location only on the way: Your live location is shared with the customer only from the moment you tap "Heading out" until you tap "Arrived". Only the latest position is stored, with no route history, and it is deleted on arrival or cancellation.

Protected contact details: Customers see your phone number only during an active job after selecting your quote, and up to 24 hours after the job ends.

Report and block: You can report or block a customer inside the app.

Private document storage: Uploaded license and insurance documents are kept in private storage and are visible only to you and the system administrator for verification. Read more in the privacy policy.
