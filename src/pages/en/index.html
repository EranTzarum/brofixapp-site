How it works

No phone calls, no searching: the pros come to you.

FAQ

How much does it cost customers?

The app is free, with no in-app purchases and no ads. You pay the pro for the job itself, according to the quote you chose.

How do I choose a pro?

Pros in your area send quotes with a price and a departure time. You compare and pick the one that suits you. Until you choose, pros see only your city, area and an approximate location; your exact address and phone go only to the pro you choose.

Which areas does BroFix cover?

BroFix is currently in a closed pilot in Israel.

How do I pay?

Directly to the pro (cash, Bit, etc.), outside the app.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account inside the app. Full details are on the account deletion page.
