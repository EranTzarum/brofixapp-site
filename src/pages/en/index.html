How it works

No calls, no searching: pros come to you.

  1. Post a request

    Describe the problem, add up to 3 photos and pick when: urgent, today, tomorrow or another day, as a time range or an exact hour.

  2. Get quotes

    Pros in your area send quotes with a price, a departure time and an estimated arrival.

  3. Choose and track

    Pick a quote, and only then does the pro get your full address. Coordinate in chat and see on the map when they arrive.

  4. Confirm and rate

    The pro taps "Done" and you confirm the job or dispute it. If you don't respond, it is confirmed automatically after 24 hours. Then you rate each other, and you can save the pros you liked.

FAQ

How much does it cost customers?

The app is free, with no in-app purchases and no ads. You pay the pro for the job itself, according to the quote you chose.

How do I choose a pro?

Pros in your area send quotes with a price, a departure time and an estimated arrival. You compare and pick the one that suits you. Until you choose, pros see only your city, area and an approximate location; your exact address and phone go only to the pro you choose.

Which areas does Brofix cover?

Brofix is currently in a closed pilot in Israel.

What if something goes wrong?

You can report or block a user inside the app, and you can email us at brofixapp@gmail.com.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account inside the app. Full details are on the account deletion page.
