A home pro, without chasing anyone

BroFix is an app that connects people who need a home-service professional with pros nearby: plumbing, electrical, locksmith, air conditioning and more.

Closed pilot in Israel
  1. Post a request: what the problem is, where, and a photo if you have one.
  2. Pros in the area send a price quote and a departure time.
  3. Pick a quote, coordinate in chat, and see on the map when the pro arrives.
  4. After the job, both sides rate each other.

Payment for the work is made directly to the professional (cash, Bit, etc.), outside the app.

BroFix is currently in a closed pilot in Israel, on Android and iPhone.
