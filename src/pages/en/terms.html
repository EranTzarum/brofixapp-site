  • The customer chooses a quote from those received. Selection creates a job between the customer and the selected professional.
  • After selection, a text chat opens for coordination, and the professional shares live location from the moment they tap "Heading out" until "Arrived". The chat is intended solely for coordinating the work.

5. Professionals

  • The professional is responsible for holding any license, permit, insurance, and qualification required by law for the work offered, and for complying with the law (including tax and labor laws).
  • Trades requiring a license: electrical, gas, and pest control. In these trades, the professional uploads a valid license or certificate in the app. Until an administrator reviews and approves the document, the professional receives no requests in that trade and cannot quote on them. If the license is rejected or removed, the trade returns to pending review.
  • In some of the other trades (such as renovation contractor, waterproofing, tiling, and drywall), an administrator approves the trade manually before requests are received.
  • License approval and the "Verified license" badge indicate only that the document was checked at the time of review. They do not constitute our guarantee of the professional's competence or work quality.
  • A quote binds the professional according to its terms. Any change in price or return visit after selection requires the customer's agreement in the app.
  • "Heading out" and "Arrived" are tapped manually by the professional. Live location is shared only with the customer for that specific job.

If the work was not completed, the customer taps "Work not completed", the job returns to in-progress status, and the case is flagged for administrator review. If the customer does not respond within 24 hours, the job is automatically confirmed as completed.
