Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 23, 2026

The Brofix website (brofixapp.com) is operated by the Brofix team. We work to make the site usable for everyone, including people with disabilities, in line with Israeli accessibility regulations for services (2013).

Standard and scope

The site is built with the goal of meeting Israeli Standard 5568, based on WCAG 2.1 Level AA. That is our target; we do not claim certification or full compliance on every point.

What we did on this site

  • Semantic HTML for headings, navigation, and content.
  • Hebrew in RTL and English in LTR, with language switching between versions.
  • Keyboard navigation and a visible focus indicator.
  • Light and dark display modes.
  • Text scales with browser zoom.
  • Alternative text (alt) for images.
  • Color contrast — targeted at WCAG 2.1 AA.
  • Promotional video: player controls, no autoplay.
  • No flashing content.

Known limitations

  • The site is new; some improvements may still be in progress.
  • Some app screenshots may not yet have a full text description.
  • Mobile app accessibility is covered separately, not in this statement.
