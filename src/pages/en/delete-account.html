Delete your BroFix account

BroFix

In the app

Open the Settings tab, tap Delete account and confirm. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Open requests are cancelled and pending quotes are withdrawn. If you have an active job (a pro on the way or a job not yet finished), you need to finish or cancel it first.

Without the app

Email brofixapp@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the subject "Delete account". We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your name, phone number, addresses, request descriptions, photos, license and insurance documents, the chat messages you wrote, replies to ratings and notifications.

What is kept

Records of completed jobs stay with the other party (price, dates and star rating) under the name "Deleted user", without your details. Records of user reports are kept for safety, without identifying details.

More details in the privacy policy.
